# BMT V31.56 — 5/7-Round Qualification Policy Hardening

## Scope
Additive Academic Challenge qualification policy engine. This release does not replace or modify ChatGPT A-owned UI/auth/payment implementation.

## Policy
- Supported qualification length: **5 or 7 rounds** only.
- Default: **7 rounds**.
- Default advancement threshold: **70%**.
- A student must have a server-derived submitted result meeting the threshold for every required round from 1 through the configured qualification round.
- Missing or below-threshold earlier rounds stop qualification progression.
- Qualification is deterministic and server-authoritative; client-supplied qualification flags are not trusted.

## Fairness lock
Qualification policy may be configured only before publication and before challenge attempts begin. Once published or attempted, the policy is locked to prevent mid-competition rule changes.

## Paid challenges
Qualification status requires a verified challenge entry for paid challenges. Free challenges do not require payment.

## Audit
Admin policy changes are recorded in `adminAuditLogs`. The new module does not create a new authoritative collection.
